📂 Game Updates (Changelog v3.0.0)

  • v3.0.0 — MANAGER MODE 🎮👔: New game mode! Create a manager (age 35-50, season 2000-01 → 2026-27, club budget recommendation per season, random Manager Score 1-99 that boosts your team). Tabs: Dashboard (team season summary + profit), Team (Stats + Formation with OVR tiles, reserve swapping, hover tooltips), Player Signing (search with market value/age/position/OVR filters, full player stats incl. career history & trophies, contract offers with acceptance chances), Sponsors (sponsors paying you with win bonus/loss cut, plus 50 sponsors you can pay for attendance boosts), Stadium (upgrades: capacity/ticket/drink/food/parking/attention/maintenance with revenue & costs), Games & Standings. Separate save slots.
  • v2.2.0: REAL FIX for the dashboard overflow — the grid computed an intrinsic width ~2300px (bigger than the screen), which clipped the Season Summary. The grid is now hard-capped to 100% width, so card + simulate match + season summary always fit in one row on any desktop screen.
  • v2.1.6: Season Summary panel width capped (~460px, anchored right) so the stat rows no longer stretch across a huge empty gap — the dashboard always fits on desktop screens.
  • v2.1.5: Season Summary panel made ~50px shorter (compact padding, tighter rows) — same fonts and content.
  • v2.1.4: Dashboard: gaps between the three sections halved (24px → 12px) and the simulate-match rectangle made 20px smaller via its padding — text and button inside unchanged.
  • v2.1.3: Dashboard: next-match panel now hugs the player card on the left (content-sized, no dead space) and the Season Summary takes the full right side — exactly per the annotated layout.
  • v2.1.2: Dashboard: next-match column now fixed at a reasonable width (can no longer balloon and push the Season Summary off-screen) — all three panels sit side by side on normal desktops.
  • v2.1.1: Dashboard layout fixed for real — the Season Summary now wraps below the card & next-match panels on narrower windows (never clipped), and the next-match panel width relaxed so it is only gently narrowed from the sides.
  • v2.1.0: Dashboard next-match panel capped in width so the Season Summary is always visible; NEW: bench contracts from superclubs (Real Madrid, Man City, Barcelona, Bayern, PSG, Liverpool, Arsenal, Inter, Juventus, Atlético, Dortmund) once you reach 80 OVR — sign and you join as a bench player until your OVR passes their starter threshold.
  • v2.0.9: Hair height increased — haircuts now keep their full natural length (no more squashed/flat hair) with width matching the head.
  • v2.0.8: Hair styles re-proportioned — width now matches the head and height restored, so all haircuts keep their natural length (no more flat wide caps).
  • v2.0.7: Head scaled down ~30% (same silhouette ratio) so it no longer fills the whole card; facial features kept larger relative to the head.
  • v2.0.6: FIXED — hair styles no longer cover the eyes (hair now sits correctly on the scalp).
  • v2.0.5: Head scaled down (same shape ratio) and facial features (eyes, mouth) enlarged so the face reads clearly.
  • v2.0.4: Head now uses the exact silhouette from the reference image (rounded top, straight sides, tapered bust) with small eyes and a curved smile.
  • v2.0.3: Head refined — rounder silhouette, smaller eyes, small curved smile.
  • v2.0.2: Player head redesigned to match the reference style — solid black round eyes and solid wide mouth (flat cartoon look).
  • v2.0.1: Player head restyled to the minimal reference design — smooth rounded head, simple dot eyes, small smile (no ears/nose).
  • v2.0.0: National tournaments moved to the end of the season; Ballon d'Or rebalance (88 OVR entry + fair scoring); realistic GK saves (shots minus goals); shot-count curve; rating penalties for bad games; contract offer negotiation; fixed social media followers/rankings; elimination-based leaderboard match counts; Appearance folder for custom player models; UI overflow fixes.

👑 Ballon d'Or Power Rankings

The live race for the prestigious **Ballon d'Or** (the World Player of the Year)! Standings and percentage chances shift dynamically after every matchday based on: player OVR, trophy success (League, Domestic Cups, UCL, World Cup, Euros/Copa), and individual match ratings.

ℹ️ HOW TO ENTER & WIN

  • Division 1 Eligibility: You must be playing for a Division 1 (Tier 1) Club. Benched or Division 2 players cannot enter the list.
  • OVR Standard: Reaching OVR >= 88 guarantees you a spot in the Top 5 rankings.
  • Silverware & Awards: Winning tournaments (UCL, Cups, Leagues, World Cup) and claiming **Golden Boot Awards** dramatically boost your percentage chance.
  • The winner is officially announced at the very end of the season campaign during the summer break!
